About the Doctor

Dr. Ananya Roy

MBBS, MD — Dermatology, Venereology & Leprology

Dr. Ananya Roy is a dermatologist and cosmetologist practising at Aura Skin & Aesthetic Clinic, Kolkata. She focuses on evidence-based skin, hair and aesthetic treatments with an emphasis on personalized care and natural-looking results.

With over 9 years of clinical experience across clinical dermatology, cosmetic dermatology and aesthetic medicine, she builds treatment plans around each patient's skin, history and long-term goals.
